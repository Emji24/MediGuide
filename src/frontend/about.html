ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making health information easier to explore.

MediGuide is a knowledge-based medical assistance project intended to present educational information about symptoms, health conditions, and general wellness.

Our purpose

MediGuide aims to help users explore general health information through an approachable interface. The project uses a structured knowledge base and rule-based reasoning as its intended foundation. The accuracy and scope of its responses depend on the knowledge and rules implemented in the system.

!

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Medical Disclaimer

MediGuide is an educational information tool only. It is not a doctor, medical provider, diagnostic service, or emergency service. Information displayed may be incomplete and may not apply to your individual circumstances.

  • Do not use MediGuide as a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.
  • Do not start, stop, or change medication or treatment based only on information from this website.
  • If symptoms are persistent, worsening, or concerning, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
  • If you think you may be experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

MediGuide does not provide a medical diagnosis or prescription.

MediGuide provides educational health information, not a medical diagnosis or prescription.
