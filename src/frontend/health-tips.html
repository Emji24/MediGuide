MEDIGUIDE WELLNESS

Healthy habits, one step at a time.

These are general wellness reminders. Choose habits that are suitable for your circumstances and follow advice from your healthcare professional.

01

Stay hydrated

Drink fluids regularly throughout the day. Your hydration needs may vary depending on your activity, environment, and health.

02

Stay active

Choose safe physical activities that suit your abilities, such as walking, stretching, or other movement you enjoy.

03

Get enough rest

Try to maintain a regular sleep schedule and create a relaxing routine before bedtime.

04

Eat balanced meals

When possible, include a variety of foods and consider any dietary guidance given by your healthcare professional.

05

Care for your well-being

Make time for rest, supportive relationships, and activities that help you manage everyday stress.

06

Attend regular checkups

Ask a qualified healthcare professional which checkups, screenings, or vaccinations are appropriate for you.

Reminder: These tips are general educational information, not personalized medical advice or treatment instructions.
MediGuide provides educational health information, not a medical diagnosis or prescription.
